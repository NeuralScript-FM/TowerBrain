## 2. Interpretation

The simulation ran using the **summer** scenario. The recorded energy consumption was 7,747.51 kWh, with a maximum 15-minute electrical demand of 180.08 kW.

The recorded comfort percentage was 99.9%. This indicates the proportion of occupied person-time within the simulator's defined comfort temperature range, when that KPI is supplied by the simulator.
